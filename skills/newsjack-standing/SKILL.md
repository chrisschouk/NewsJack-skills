# Standing check

The question every reactive post must answer first: is this story yours to speak
on?

## When to use

- Before any reactive or newsjacking post.
- When a client asks "can we jump on this?"
- When a draft makes a claim about ownership, discovery, or being first.

## Procedure

Ask three questions and require a yes to at least two.

1. **Connection.** Does the brand have a real, current relationship to the subject:
   a client, a release, a campaign, or a documented position?
2. **Contribution.** Can the brand add something the story does not already say:
   context, a counterpoint, a concrete example, a result?
3. **Permission.** Is there a person, artist, or client who has agreed to be
   associated with this take?

## Verdicts

- **Two or three yes.** Standing confirmed. Proceed to `newsjack-angle`.
- **One yes.** Weak standing. Say so. Offer a narrower take the brand can defend.
- **Zero yes.** No standing. Refuse, and explain what would create it.

## Hard refusals

- A brand claiming a story is "ours" when it is not.
- A post that implies a client relationship that does not exist.
- A take that needs a quote nobody gave.

## Guardrails

- Never soften a zero into a maybe. The refusal is the value.
- Never accept "it will do numbers" as standing.
- State the reason in one sentence, then stop.

## Output

The verdict, the three answers, and either the next step or what would create
standing.
